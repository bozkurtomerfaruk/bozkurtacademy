Ömer Faruk Bozkurt

I study Mechanical Engineering in Germany and teach German online, one-to-one and in groups, from A1 to B2.

We work with clear explanations, regular review and plenty of speaking practice.

I bring my experience of life in Germany into the lessons through real examples.

My education and work experience

Language teaching, engineering and Germany.

Experience as a tutor

I have been giving language lessons since my high school years.

Engineering studies

I began my studies at Istanbul Technical University, continued them at Hochschule Düsseldorf with Erasmus+ and now study Mechanical Engineering at the University of Duisburg-Essen.

How did I learn German?

I learned German in Germany, at school and at work; I am confident in both everyday and academic German.

Languages

My native language is Turkish; I speak German and English fluently.

Living and working in Germany

I have gained experience in production, quality, processes and data analysis at Mercedes-Benz, Aluminium Norf, Ford and TÜBİTAK.

If you are planning to move to Germany, we can practise these situations in advance:

  • making appointments and writing formal emails
  • introducing yourself in a job interview
  • giving your opinion in a meeting

If you work in a technical field, we can also work on technical German.

Languages I use in lessons
