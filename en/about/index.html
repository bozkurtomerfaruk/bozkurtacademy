Ömer Faruk Bozkurt

I study Mechanical Engineering in Germany and teach German online as Bozkurt Academy – from A1 to B2, one-to-one or in small groups.

We speak German in class. When something needs explaining, I explain it in English, so you understand the rule and keep moving.

I learned German here, at university and at work, and I bring that everyday experience into the lessons.

Who are the lessons for?

Living in Germany

You live or work in Germany and want to handle everyday life, appointments and work in German with more confidence.

Moving to Germany

You are preparing for a job, a degree or a move and want to arrive with solid German.

Exams

You need a certificate: Goethe, telc, ÖSD, DTZ or TestDaF.

Education and experience

Teaching

I have been teaching languages since high school.

University

I started at Istanbul Technical University (İTÜ), continued at Hochschule Düsseldorf with Erasmus+, and now study Mechanical Engineering at the University of Duisburg-Essen.

Learned German in Germany

I learned German at university and at work and use both everyday and academic German.

Languages

Turkish is my native language; I speak German and English fluently.

Work experience in Germany

At Mercedes-Benz, Aluminium Norf, Ford and TÜBİTAK I worked in production, quality, processes and data analysis.

  • giving your opinion in meetings

Languages in class

Explain, use, repeat

  1. Plan

    Lessons follow your level and your goal, for example an exam date or a new job.

  2. Use

    You use new structures straight away, in speaking and writing.

  3. Repeat

    We come back to difficult topics. The website has free exercises for this.
