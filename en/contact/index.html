Contact and free first lesson

Online German lessons, group lessons or exam preparation: send me a message using the form. It comes straight to me.

Useful things to mention

  • your German level (A1–B2), if you know it – otherwise try the free placement test first
  • your goal: everyday life, an exam, work or studies
  • what you find hardest
  • for an exam: its name and date
  • one-to-one or group lessons
  • days and times that suit you, with your time zone

Email: info@bozkurtacademy.com

Contact form

Your name, email address and message are forwarded to me by email via Web3Forms (web3forms.com). Details are in the privacy policy.

What happens next?

  1. I read your message and reply by email as soon as I can.
  2. We pick a day and time for your free first lesson.
  3. In that lesson we talk about your level and goals and choose the type of lessons and a plan. I share the prices at this point too.
