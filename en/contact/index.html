Contact and free first lesson

Fill in the form for online German lessons, group classes, exam preparation or information about fees. Your message comes straight to me.

What you can include in your message

  • Your German level (A1–B2, if you know it). If you don’t, you can take the placement test first.
  • Your goal: everyday German, an exam, work or school
  • A topic or skill you find difficult
  • The name and date of your exam, if you have one
  • Whether you prefer one-to-one or group lessons
  • The days and times that suit you

Email: bozkurtt.omerfaruk@gmail.com

Contact form

Your name, email address and message are forwarded to me by email via Web3Forms (web3forms.com). Details are in the privacy policy.

What happens after you send your message?

  1. I read your message and reply by email. Messages are answered as soon as possible.
  2. Together we choose a day and time for your free first lesson.
  3. In the first lesson we talk about your level and your goal and decide on the right type of lesson and a plan for you. I also share information about fees at this stage.

If your question isn’t answered here, have a look at the frequently asked questions.
