Frequently Asked Questions

The most common questions about the online German lessons, lesson formats, prices, exams and the free material on the website.

Lessons

How do the lessons work?

Online, on Zoom or Google Meet. All you need is a computer, tablet or phone with a camera and microphone and a stable internet connection. More on the online German lessons page.

How long is a lesson?

50 minutes. We agree on days and times in our first conversation; just tell me your time zone.

Is the first lesson really free?

Yes. In the first lesson we talk about your level, your goal and what you find hard, and choose a lesson format and plan. Taking it doesn’t commit you to anything.

Do you explain things in English?

Yes. We practise in German, and I explain new rules and tricky points in English whenever that helps.

Which levels do you teach?

From A1 to B2: A1, A2, B1 and B2.

One-to-one and group lessons

What is the difference between one-to-one and group lessons?

In one-to-one lessons the content, pace and schedule are planned entirely around you. In a group, up to 6 learners at the same level follow a fixed programme and practise speaking with each other too. Compare the private lessons and group lessons pages.

Which one suits me?

If you have an exam date or a specific goal, one-to-one lessons usually get you there faster. If you want a regular programme and to learn with others, a group is a good choice. Not sure? We decide together in the free first lesson.

Can I take lessons with a friend or my partner?

Yes, if your levels are similar. We then plan the lessons as a small group of up to 6 people. Just mention it in your message.

Prices and planning

How much do lessons cost?

It depends on whether you choose one-to-one or group lessons, how many lessons you take per week and whether there is a special goal such as exam preparation. That’s why there is no fixed price list on the website. For a plan and prices, use the contact form.

How many lessons a week should I take?

We decide together based on your level and goal – if your exam is coming up soon, for example, we plan around it. We make the decision in our first conversation.

What if I need to reschedule or cancel a lesson?

Just let me know in advance and we’ll find a new time.

Please note: lessons rescheduled or cancelled within 24 hours of the start are charged, and lessons can only continue once that fee is paid. This keeps the reserved times fair for everyone.

Exams and levels

I don’t know my level. Where should I start?

Take the free placement test on the website (5 to 25 minutes); the result is sent to me. We can also find your level together in the free first lesson.

Which exams can I prepare for?

Goethe-Zertifikat, telc Deutsch, ÖSD, DTZ and TestDaF, as well as school and university exams. More on the exam preparation page.

Which level do I need for family reunification?

The level, the accepted certificates and any exceptions depend on your application. Check the current information on the official website of the relevant German mission before you apply. The German course A1 page explains how we prepare for the A1 exam.

Which level do I need to work in Germany?

That depends on the profession, the employer and the process. Always check the required level with the employer or the relevant institution. What each level covers is described on the B1 and B2 pages.

Free material

Are the exercises on the website free?

Yes. The exercises and exam practice sets are free for everyone. The menus are in English; the grammar lessons and the explanations under the answers are currently in Turkish.

Do I need an account for the exercises?

No. You can use the exercises and practice sets without an account.

Didn’t find your question?

Send me a message and I’ll reply as soon as I can. The first lesson is free.
