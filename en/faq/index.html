Frequently Asked Questions

The most common questions about online German lessons, lesson formats, how fees are set, exams and the free materials on this site.

Lessons

How do the lessons take place?

Lessons take place online via Zoom or Google Meet. A computer, tablet or phone with a camera and microphone and a stable internet connection are all you need. You will find the details on the online German lessons page.

How long is a lesson?

A lesson lasts 50 minutes. We plan the lesson days and times together in our first conversation.

Is the first lesson really free?

Yes. In the first lesson we talk about your level, your goal and the topics you find difficult, and we decide on the right type of lesson and a plan for you. Attending this lesson doesn’t commit you to any further lessons.

Which levels do you teach?

I teach every level from A1 to B2: A1, A2, B1 and B2.

One-to-one and group lessons

What is the difference between one-to-one lessons and group classes?

In one-to-one lessons, the content, pace and lesson times are planned entirely around you. In group classes you progress with a fixed schedule in a group of up to 6 learners at the same level and also practise speaking with the other participants. For a comparison, see the private German lessons and German group classes pages.

Which suits me better?

If you have a specific exam date or a particular goal, one-to-one lessons usually help you progress faster. If you want a regular schedule and to learn together with others, group classes are a good choice. If you are not sure, we can decide together in the free first lesson.

Can I take lessons together with a friend or my partner?

Yes, if your levels are similar. In that case we can plan the lessons as a small group class. Groups have no more than 6 people. Just mention it in your message.

Fees and planning

How much do the lessons cost?

The fee depends on whether you choose one-to-one or group lessons, the number of lessons per week and whether you have a specific goal such as exam preparation. That is why there is no fixed price list on the site. For a plan and fee information that suit you, write to me via the contact form.

What affects the fee?

The three things that affect the fee most are the lesson format (one-to-one or group), the number of lessons per week and the overall lesson plan. For exam preparation, the time left until the exam also affects the plan.

How many lessons a week should I take?

We decide on the number of lessons per week together, based on your level and your goal. If you have an exam coming up soon, for example, we plan accordingly. We make this decision together in our first conversation.

What happens if I need to reschedule or cancel a lesson?

If you need to change a lesson time, just let me know in advance and we will find a new day and time together.

Please note: for lessons rescheduled or cancelled within 24 hours of the start, the fee for that lesson is charged. Lessons cannot continue until it has been paid. This rule keeps the lesson times I reserve fair for everyone.

Exams and levels

I don’t know my level. Where should I start?

You can take the free placement test on this site. The test takes between 5 and 25 minutes and the result is sent to me. If you prefer, we can also determine your level together in the free first lesson.

Which exams do you prepare learners for?

We prepare for the Goethe-Zertifikat, telc Deutsch, ÖSD, DTZ and TestDaF exams as well as for school exams. You will find the details on the exam preparation page.

Which level is required for family reunification?

The required level, the accepted certificates and any exceptions can vary depending on the type of application. Before you apply, check the latest information on the official website of the responsible embassy or consulate. On the A1 German course page I explain how we prepare for the A1 exam.

Which level do I need to work in Germany?

That depends on the profession, the employer and the process. Always check the required level with the employer or the relevant institution. You will find the lesson content for these levels on the B1 and B2 pages.

Free materials

Are the grammar topics and exercises on this site free?

Yes. The grammar explanations, exercises and exam practice sets are open to everyone and free of charge. Every exercise question comes with an explanation of the answer. The grammar explanations and exercise explanations are in Turkish.

Do I need an account to do the exercises?

No. You can use the exercises, grammar explanations and practice sets without creating an account.

Didn’t find your answer here?

Write to me and I will reply as soon as possible. The first lesson is free.
