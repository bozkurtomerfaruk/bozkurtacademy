German Course B2

B2 is the level where you use German comfortably at work, in your profession and at university. The B2 course covers discussion, formal writing and complex sentence structures, based on your goal.

What can you do at B2?

B2 is the second step of the CEFR’s “independent user” level. At B2 you understand the main ideas of complex texts on abstract topics, follow technical discussions in your field and talk with native speakers without strain on either side. You write clear, detailed texts and weigh up the pros and cons of different options.

The step from B1 to B2 is usually this: you already know what to say – now you learn to say it more precisely and appropriately. For example:

  • Je mehr ich spreche, desto sicherer werde ich. (The more I speak, the more confident I get.)
  • Der Antrag muss bis Freitag eingereicht werden. (The application must be submitted by Friday.)
  • Der Sprecher sagte, das Projekt sei erfolgreich gewesen. (The spokesperson said the project had been successful.)

Who needs B2?

B2 matters for anyone who wants to use German more fluently and accurately at work, in their profession or at university. Which level and certificate are required for jobs, professional recognition, vocational training or university admission depends on the institution, the federal state and the profession – always check the current requirements before you apply.

If you work in engineering or a technical field, we can also practise technical German: the language of meetings, reports and emails and the vocabulary of your field. I study engineering in Germany and have worked in production, quality and technical projects, so I can use real examples.

What you work on in the B2 course

Advanced grammar

  • Konjunktiv I: reported speech
  • The passive with modal verbs
  • Partizip I and Partizip II
  • je … desto

Formal and academic German

  • Nominalisation
  • Prepositions with the genitive
  • Formal emails and applications
  • Describing charts and tables

Speaking and discussion

  • Giving your opinion and answering counter-arguments
  • Presenting and answering questions
  • Job interviews and meetings
  • Taking notes on longer listening texts

Preparing for B2 exams

Goethe-Zertifikat B2 and telc Deutsch B2 are the most common exams at this level. Both have reading, listening, writing and speaking sections; telc B2 also has a “Sprachbausteine” section testing grammar and vocabulary.

We practise every task type against the clock and revise your writing against the marking criteria. There are free practice sets for the Goethe B2 reading section and the telc B2 reading and Sprachbausteine sections. More on the exam preparation page.

You can take the lessons one-to-one or in a group. For B2 exam preparation, one-to-one lessons are usually more effective, because personal feedback on writing and speaking matters so much. For prices, just get in touch.

Questions about the B2 course

Why does the step from B1 to B2 feel so hard?

At B2 you don’t just learn new rules – you learn to express the same idea in different, more formal ways, and your vocabulary has to grow fast. Regular reading, listening and writing are especially important.

Can I study at a German university with B2?

Language requirements depend on the programme and the university. Check your university’s own website; in class we plan around your goal.

Can I start technical German before B2?

Yes. Field-specific vocabulary and correspondence can be added from B1. At B2 we move on to meetings, reports and presentations.
