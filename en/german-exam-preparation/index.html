Why does exam preparation need its own work?

Knowing German and passing a German exam are not always the same thing. Exams are made up of specific task types, each part has a time limit and answers are assessed according to set criteria.

Even if your level is good enough, you lose points if you don’t know the task types and the timing. That is why in the lessons we:

  • close gaps in grammar and vocabulary,
  • practise every part against the clock,
  • learn ready-to-use phrases for writing and speaking.

Which exams can you prepare for?

Goethe-Zertifikat

We prepare for Goethe exams from Start Deutsch 1 at A1 up to B2.

telc Deutsch

From A1 to C1; at B1 and B2 there is a “Sprachbausteine” part that tests grammar.

ÖSD

The Austria-based ÖSD exams are also used to certify German levels; their task types are similar to Goethe and telc.

DTZ

Deutsch-Test für Zuwanderer: the exam for immigrants in Germany.

TestDaF

An exam related to university applications; check the requirement with the university you are applying to. Reading practice sets are available on the site.

School exams

If you take German at school or university, we prepare for written and oral exams by reviewing the topics.

How do we prepare for the four parts of the exam?

Lesen

Reading

You learn to read with the question in mind and practise against the clock with the reading practice sets.

Hören

Listening

We practise reading the question in advance, catching the key word and matching it to the options.

Schreiben

Writing

You build a clear structure for emails and opinion texts, and we correct your text according to the criteria.

Sprechen

Speaking

We rehearse introductions, planning tasks and presentations under exam conditions.

How is an exam preparation plan put together?

  1. Free first conversation

    We talk about the exam and the date. If you don’t know your level, you can take the free placement test first.

  2. Identifying gaps

    With a short mock test we find your weakest part and build the plan around it.

  3. Working on task types

    In each lesson we work on one or two task types; you close gaps with the grammar explanations and exercises (in Turkish).

  4. Exam rehearsal

    In the final weeks we do full timed mock exams and rehearse the speaking part.

Questions about exam preparation

Which is easier, Goethe or telc?

Both exams measure the same CEFR levels; their task types and structure differ slightly. Which one suits you better depends on where you will use the certificate and on the exam dates. Check in advance which exams are accepted by the institution you will submit the certificate to.

How far in advance should I start preparing?

If your current level is close to the exam level, a few weeks of intensive preparation may be enough. If your level is lower, we first complete the topics of that level. The earlier you start, the more relaxed the plan will be.

Are the practice sets on this site official exam questions?

No. The practice sets are exercises based on the task types of the official exams. They help you get to know the exam format and practise against the clock.

Do you register me for the exam?

No. You register for the exam yourself with the exam provider or an authorised exam centre. In the lessons you prepare for the exam.

Is exam preparation done in one-to-one or group lessons?

Because personal feedback is very important for the writing and speaking tasks, exam preparation usually takes place in private one-to-one lessons. For general level work there are also group classes.

How can I use the free practice sets?

You can do the exam practice sets on this site without creating an account. In the reading and grammar parts you get to know the task types and practise against the clock.
