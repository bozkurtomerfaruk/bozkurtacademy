Why exam preparation needs its own focus

Knowing German and passing a German exam are not always the same thing. Exams are made of fixed task types, each part has a time limit, and answers are marked against set criteria.

Even at the right level you lose points if you don’t know the task types and the timing. So in the lessons we:

  • close gaps in grammar and vocabulary,
  • practise every part of the exam against the clock,
  • learn useful phrases for writing and speaking.

Which exams can you prepare for?

Goethe-Zertifikat

From Start Deutsch 1 (A1) up to B2.

telc Deutsch

From A1 to C1; at B1 and B2 there is a “Sprachbausteine” section that tests grammar.

ÖSD

The Austrian Language Diploma; its task types are similar to Goethe and telc.

DTZ

The Deutsch-Test für Zuwanderer, the final exam of the German integration course.

TestDaF

Used for university applications – check your university’s requirements. Reading practice sets are on the website.

School and university

Taking German at school or university? We prepare for written and oral exams by reviewing the material.

How the plan is built

  1. Free first conversation

    We talk about the exam and the date. If you don’t know your level, take the free placement test first.

  2. Finding the gaps

    A short test shows your weakest part, and the plan is built around it.

  3. Working on task types

    One or two task types per lesson; you close gaps in between with the exercises on the website.

  4. Dress rehearsal

    In the last weeks: a full timed mock exam and a speaking rehearsal.

Exam preparation usually takes place in private lessons; the price depends on the length and the number of lessons per week. For details, use the contact form.

Questions about exam preparation

Is Goethe or telc easier?

Both test the same CEFR levels; their task types and structure differ a little. Which suits you better depends on where you will use the certificate and when exam dates are available. Check in advance which exams the institution you are applying to accepts.

How early should I start preparing?

If your level is close to the exam level, a few weeks of intensive preparation may be enough. If it is lower, we first cover the topics of that level. The earlier you start, the more relaxed the plan.

Are the practice sets on the website real exam papers?

No. They are our own exercises based on the task types of the official exams. They help you get to know the format and practise against the clock.

Do you register me for the exam?

No. You register yourself with the exam provider or an authorised test centre. The lessons prepare you for it.

Is exam preparation one-to-one or in a group?

Because personal feedback matters so much in writing and speaking, usually one-to-one. For general level work there are also group lessons.

How do I use the free practice sets?

You can do the practice sets without an account. In the reading and grammar sections you get to know the task types and practise against the clock.
