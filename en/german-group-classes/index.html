The advantages of an online German group course

One of the biggest sources of motivation when learning a language is seeing other people on the same path. In a group you also learn from other people’s questions: a question you hadn’t thought of gets answered because someone else asked it. You hear different accents and different ways of building sentences, and you practise in a setting that is closer to a real conversation.

Groups have no more than 6 people, so everyone takes an active part and gets the chance to speak in every lesson. Group classes follow a fixed schedule. “I didn’t get round to studying this week” becomes harder to say, because the group meets on the same day and at the same time every week. For learners who find it hard to study regularly, this structure is very valuable.

An online group course offers the advantages of a classroom course without travel or lost time. You can join from anywhere, whether you live in Türkiye, in Germany or elsewhere. Lessons take place via Zoom or Google Meet.

What do we do in group classes?

Explanations

We learn the grammar topics of your level with clear examples. At A1, for instance, we look at verb conjugation with sentences such as “Ich lerne Deutsch.” (I am learning German.)

Pair and group conversations

Together with the other participants, we act out situations such as introducing yourself, making an appointment, giving directions or sharing your opinion on a topic.

Listening and reading

With short texts and dialogues we expand your vocabulary and practise expressing what you have understood.

Review and homework

Between lessons you review the topics with the grammar explanations and exercises on this site (in Turkish).

What happens in a group lesson?

  1. Warm-up

    At the start of the lesson we talk briefly in German about the past week. Everyone says a few sentences.

  2. New topic

    I explain the new structure with simple examples. Because questions are answered in front of everyone, you also learn from other people’s questions.

  3. Practising together

    We use the new structure in pair conversations, short role plays and shared writing tasks.

  4. Homework and review

    At the end of the lesson we discuss what you should review before the next lesson and which exercises you will do.

Group classes or one-to-one lessons?

Group classes and one-to-one lessons cover the same levels and use the same method. Which one suits you depends on your goal, your schedule and the way you like to learn.

If your exam date is close or you want a plan made entirely for you, have a look at the private one-to-one German lessons page.

Who are group classes for?

  • Beginners who want to complete level A1 with a regular schedule
  • Learners at A2 or B1 level who want more speaking practice
  • People who lose motivation when studying alone and would like to progress with a group
  • People who want to learn German together with a friend, their partner or their family

For a group to work well, the participants’ levels need to be close to one another. That is why we determine your level before you join a group. You can take the free placement test for this, or we can determine your level together in our first conversation.

How are group class fees set?

The fee for group classes depends on the length of the programme and the number of lessons per week. We decide on the number of lessons per week together, based on the level and the goal. For information about the groups and fees, write to me via the contact form.

Questions about German group classes

How are the groups put together?

Groups are formed by level and have no more than 6 people. When the participants’ levels are close, everyone can take an active part and the topics progress at a suitable pace for everyone.

Will I get a chance to speak in a group class?

Yes. We deliberately set aside time for pair and group conversations, so everyone gets to speak and practise.

What happens if I miss a lesson?

You can catch up on the topic you missed with the grammar explanations and exercises on this site (in Turkish). I will also tell you what to study so that you are ready for the next lesson.

Can I switch from group classes to one-to-one lessons?

Yes. If your goal changes, for example because you now have an exam date, you can switch to one-to-one lessons or take one-to-one lessons in addition to your group class.
