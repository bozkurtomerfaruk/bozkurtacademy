Why learn in a group?

Seeing other people on the same path is one of the best motivators. In a group you also learn from other people’s questions – often someone asks exactly what you hadn’t thought of. You hear different accents and different ways of building sentences, which is much closer to a real conversation.

Groups have at most 6 people, so everyone takes part and gets to speak in every lesson. Because the group meets at the same time every week, it’s much harder to skip a week – a real help if you struggle to study regularly on your own.

Online means no commute and no wasted time. You can join from anywhere; lessons take place on Zoom or Google Meet.

What we do in the group

Grammar made clear

The topics of your level with clear examples – at A1, for instance, verb endings with sentences like “Ich lerne Deutsch.” (I’m learning German.)

Speaking in pairs and as a group

Introducing yourself, making an appointment, giving directions or giving your opinion – we act out real situations together.

Listening and reading

Short texts and dialogues build your vocabulary and help you say what you understood.

Review and homework

Between lessons you review with the exercises on the website.

What a group lesson looks like

  1. Warm-up

    A short chat about the past week in German – everyone says a few sentences.

  2. New topic

    I introduce the new structure with simple examples. Questions are answered for everyone, so you learn from each other’s questions.

  3. Practise together

    Pair conversations, short role plays and writing together.

  4. Homework and review

    At the end we agree on what to review before the next lesson and which exercises to do.

Group or one-to-one?

Both follow the same levels and the same method. Which one suits you depends on your goal, your schedule and how you like to learn.

Who are group lessons for?

  • beginners who want to complete A1 with a regular programme
  • A2 or B1 learners who want more speaking practice
  • anyone who loses motivation studying alone
  • friends, couples or family members who want to learn German together

A group works best when everyone is at a similar level, so we find your level before you join – with the free placement test or in our first conversation.

How is the price set?

It depends on the length of the programme and the number of lessons per week, which we decide based on level and goal. For information about groups and prices, use the contact form.

Questions about group lessons

How are the groups formed?

By level, with at most 6 people. When everyone is at a similar level, everyone can take part and the pace suits the whole group.

Will I get enough chances to speak?

Yes. We set aside time for pair and group conversations, so everyone speaks in every lesson.

What if I miss a lesson?

You can catch up with the exercises on the website, and I’ll tell you what to review before the next lesson.

Can I switch from group to one-to-one lessons?

Yes. If your goal changes – for example, you get an exam date – you can switch or add one-to-one lessons alongside the group.
