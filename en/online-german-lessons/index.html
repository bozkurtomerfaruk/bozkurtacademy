Why do online German lessons work?

The hardest part of learning German is often studying regularly. With online lessons there is no travelling and no waiting, so it is easy to keep a steady routine.

We correct sentences together on screen and you keep your notes. Speaking practice is just as live as in a face-to-face lesson.

You see German not only as it appears in textbooks but also as it is used in everyday life in Germany (making appointments, writing emails, job interviews and more). To get started, have a look at the 40 phrases in the post everyday German phrases (in Turkish).

How the lessons work, in short

  • Lessons last 50 minutes.
  • The first lesson is free; together we identify your level and your goal.
  • The content is prepared for you; we don’t simply work through a textbook page by page.
  • Between lessons you review with the exercises on this site.

How do the lessons progress?

  1. Level and goal

    In the free first lesson we talk about your level, your goal and the topics you find difficult. If you like, you can take the free placement test first.

  2. Personal lesson plan

    Family reunification, work, university or vocational training (Ausbildung): we set the order of topics and the pace according to your goal.

  3. Explanation and practice

    In every lesson you learn a new structure with examples and then reinforce it through dialogues and writing tasks.

  4. Review and progress checks

    We keep earlier topics fresh with short reviews. Between lessons you can read the grammar explanations and do exercises for your level.

One-to-one or group lessons?

Private one-to-one German lessons follow your goal and your pace entirely; if you have an exam date, they are usually the best option. German group classes take place with learners at the same level and give you regular speaking practice.

If you are not sure, we decide together in the free first lesson.

Who are the lessons for?

  • People planning to move to Germany
  • Anyone preparing for a German exam for family reunification or a visa application
  • Students who want to study at a university in Germany
  • People considering vocational training (Ausbildung)
  • Students going to Germany with Erasmus or another exchange programme
  • Engineers, healthcare workers and other professionals who want to work in Germany
  • People living in Germany who want to improve their German
  • Anyone preparing for the Goethe, telc, ÖSD, DTZ or TestDaF exam
  • Pupils and students taking German at school or university
  • Learners who lack speaking practice and want to use German with confidence

We can also work on technical German for engineering and the workplace. If you are preparing for an exam, you will find the details on the exam preparation page.

What do you need for an online lesson?

A computer or tablet with a camera and microphone (or a phone if necessary) and a stable internet connection are all you need. You don’t have to buy any software.

How are lesson fees set?

The fee depends on the type of lesson (one-to-one or group), the number of lessons per week and any specific goal such as exam preparation. For details, write to me via the contact form; the first lesson is free.

Questions about online German lessons

Are online German lessons as effective as face-to-face lessons?

Yes, if you attend regularly. You speak live, we correct things together on screen, and you don’t lose any time travelling.

I don’t speak any German. Can I start online lessons?

Yes. Level A1 is for complete beginners.

How long are the lessons and which software do you use?

A lesson lasts 50 minutes and takes place via Zoom or Google Meet. We agree on days and times together in our first conversation.

I don’t know my level. What should I do?

You can take the free placement test on this site in 5 to 25 minutes; the result is sent to me. If you prefer, we can also determine your level together in the first lesson.

Should I choose one-to-one or group lessons?

If you have a specific exam date or a particular goal, one-to-one lessons are usually the better choice. If you want a regular schedule and to learn together with others, group classes of up to 6 people are a good option. You will find more details on the private lessons and group classes pages.

What happens if I need to reschedule or cancel a lesson?

Just let me know in advance and we will find a new day and time. For lessons rescheduled or cancelled within 24 hours of the start, the fee for that lesson is charged; lessons cannot continue until it has been paid.

Which exams can I prepare for?

You can prepare for the Goethe, telc, ÖSD, DTZ and TestDaF exams as well as for school exams. More details are on the exam preparation page.

How can I review between lessons?

You can read the grammar explanations on this site and do the exercises. Every exercise question comes with an explanation of the answer.
