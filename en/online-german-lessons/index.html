Why online lessons work

The hardest part of learning German is usually keeping at it. With no commute, a fixed weekly lesson is much easier to keep.

We correct sentences together on screen, your notes stay saved, and speaking practice is just as live as in a classroom.

You also learn the German people actually use in Germany – making appointments, writing emails, going to a job interview – not only the textbook version.

In short

  • Lessons last 50 minutes.
  • The first lesson is free; we find your level and goal together.
  • Content is planned for you, not page by page through a textbook.
  • Between lessons you review with the exercises on the website.

How the lessons work

  1. Level and goal

    In the free first lesson we talk about your level, your goal and what you find hard. You can take the free placement test beforehand.

  2. Your own plan

    Work, university, a visa or an exam: the order of topics and the pace follow your goal.

  3. Explain and practise

    Each lesson introduces a new structure with examples, then you use it in conversations and short texts.

  4. Review

    Short reviews keep earlier topics fresh. Between lessons you can practise with exercises for your level.

One-to-one or group?

Private lessons follow your goal and pace completely, which usually makes them the best choice if you have an exam date. Group lessons bring together learners at the same level and give you regular speaking practice.

Not sure? We decide together in the free first lesson.

Who are the lessons for?

  • People planning to move to Germany
  • Anyone who needs a German certificate for a visa or family reunification
  • Students who want to study at a German university
  • Erasmus and exchange students
  • Engineers, healthcare workers and other professionals who want to work in Germany
  • People living in Germany who want to improve their German
  • Anyone preparing for Goethe, telc, ÖSD, DTZ or TestDaF
  • Learners who know the grammar but need speaking practice

If you work in engineering or a technical field, we can also practise technical German. Preparing for an exam? See exam preparation.

What do you need?

A computer, a tablet or, if necessary, a phone with a camera and microphone, plus a stable internet connection. You don’t need to buy any software.

How is the price set?

It depends on the format (one-to-one or group), the number of lessons per week and any special goal such as exam preparation. Send me a message using the contact form; the first lesson is free.

Questions about online German lessons

Are online lessons as effective as in-person lessons?

Yes, if you attend regularly. You speak live, we correct together on screen, and you don’t lose time travelling.

I don’t speak any German. Can I start?

Yes. A1 is for complete beginners, and I explain new grammar in English.

How long is a lesson, and which platform do you use?

A lesson lasts 50 minutes and takes place on Zoom or Google Meet. We agree on days and times in the first conversation; let me know your time zone.

What if I need to reschedule or cancel a lesson?

Just let me know in advance and we’ll find a new time. Lessons rescheduled or cancelled within 24 hours of the start are charged, and lessons can only continue once that fee is paid.

Which exams can I prepare for?

Goethe, telc, ÖSD, DTZ and TestDaF. Details are on the exam preparation page.

How do I review between lessons?

With the free exercises on the website, sorted by level and topic. The menus are in English; the short explanations under the answers are currently in Turkish, so in class we go through any mistakes together.
