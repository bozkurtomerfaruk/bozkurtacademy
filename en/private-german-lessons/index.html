Who are private lessons for?

In a group everyone works on the same topic at the same speed. That suits some people, but not everyone. If you have an exam date, a changing work schedule or a topic you keep getting stuck on, one-to-one lessons usually get you there faster.

You never wait for your turn to ask a question. If a rule doesn’t click, I explain it again with other examples – in English if that helps. You don’t sit through things you already know, and almost all of the speaking time is yours, which makes a big difference if speaking makes you nervous.

Private lessons work especially well when:

  • you have an exam date: we plan backwards from your Goethe, telc, ÖSD or DTZ exam. See exam preparation.
  • you need German for a visa or family reunification: we work towards the certificate you need.
  • you need German for work: job interviews, emails or technical vocabulary.
  • you are moving to or living in Germany: appointments, letters from the authorities, small talk at work.
  • speaking is your weak point: if you know the grammar but freeze when speaking, most of the lesson is speaking.

What makes these lessons different

A teacher who lives in Germany

I live and study in Germany and bring real examples from university, work and everyday life into the lessons.

A plan made for you

We don’t work through a textbook page by page. Your goal and your progress decide how much time a topic gets.

Practice between lessons

The exercises on the website are free and sorted by level and topic; we go through your mistakes together in class.

What a private lesson looks like

  1. Quick review

    A few questions and sentences on last time’s topic; if there was homework, we go through it.

  2. New topic

    I introduce the new structure with simple examples. For the Perfekt, for instance, we start with short sentences like “Ich habe gegessen.” (I have eaten.) and then you describe your own day.

  3. Practice

    You use the structure in speaking and writing. I correct mistakes straight away and explain why.

  4. Next step

    At the end we agree on what to review and which exercises to do.

One-to-one and group lessons compared

Both follow the same levels and the same method. The difference is pace, flexibility and speaking time.

What does the price depend on?

The number of lessons per week, the overall plan and whether special preparation such as an exam is needed. Before you commit to anything, the first lesson is free, so you can see whether the lessons suit you. For prices, just send a message through the contact form.

Questions about private lessons

How many lessons a week should I take?

We decide together based on your level and goal – if your exam is coming up soon, for example, we plan around it. We make the decision in our first conversation.

Will you explain things in English?

Yes, whenever it helps. We practise in German, but new rules and tricky differences can be explained in English.

What if I need to reschedule or cancel a lesson?

Just let me know in advance and we’ll find a new time.

Please note: lessons rescheduled or cancelled within 24 hours of the start are charged, and lessons can only continue once that fee is paid. This keeps the reserved times fair for everyone.

Do you use teaching materials?

Yes: explanations, example sentences and exercises. The exercises on the website are free for reviewing between lessons.
