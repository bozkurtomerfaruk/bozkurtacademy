Who are private one-to-one German lessons for?

In group classes, everyone works on the same topic at the same pace. That suits some learners well, but not everyone. If you have a fixed exam date, if your working hours change often or if you are stuck on a particular topic, one-to-one lessons will get you results faster.

In a one-to-one lesson you never have to wait your turn to ask a question. If a rule isn’t clear, we look at the same topic again with different examples, and you don’t have to sit through topics you already know. Almost all of the speaking time is yours, which makes a big difference for learners who are hesitant to speak.

Private one-to-one German lessons are particularly suitable if:

  • You have an exam date: we make a plan based on the time left until your Goethe, telc, ÖSD or DTZ exam. You will find the details on the exam preparation page.
  • You need a German certificate for family reunification or a visa: on the A1 German course page I explain how we prepare for the A1 exam.
  • You need German for your job: we focus on job interviews, emails or technical vocabulary.
  • You need support at school or university: we go through your course topics step by step and prepare for assignments and exams.
  • You lack speaking practice: if you know the grammar but get stuck when speaking, we spend most of the lesson talking.

What happens in a private lesson?

  1. Quick review

    We recall the topic of the previous lesson with a few questions and sentences. If I gave you homework, we go through it together.

  2. New topic

    I explain the new structure with simple examples. When learning the Perfekt, for instance, we start with short sentences such as “Ich habe gegessen.” (I have eaten.) and then move on to describing your own day.

  3. Practice

    You use the new structure by speaking and writing. I correct mistakes as they happen and explain why something is wrong.

  4. Next steps

    At the end of the lesson we discuss what you should review and which exercises you can do.

One-to-one lessons and group classes compared

Both formats cover the same levels and use the same method. The differences are pace, flexibility and speaking time.

What does the fee for private lessons depend on?

The fee for one-to-one lessons depends on the number of lessons per week, the overall lesson plan and whether you need special preparation, for example for an exam. Before you commit to a package, we hold the first lesson free of charge, so you can see whether the lessons suit you. For fees, simply write to me via the contact form.

Questions about private German lessons

How many lessons a week should I take?

We decide on the number of lessons per week together, based on your level and your goal. If you have an exam coming up soon, for example, we plan accordingly. We make this decision together in our first conversation.

What happens if I need to reschedule or cancel a lesson?

If you need to change a lesson time, just let me know in advance and we will find a new day and time together.

Please note: for lessons rescheduled or cancelled within 24 hours of the start, the fee for that lesson is charged. Lessons cannot continue until it has been paid. This rule keeps the lesson times I reserve fair for everyone.

Which is faster, private lessons or a course?

In the same amount of time, one-to-one lessons usually give you more speaking practice and more time for the topics you find difficult. That is why private lessons often help you progress faster if you have a specific goal. If you want regular practice and to learn together with others, group classes are also a good choice.

Do you use learning materials?

Yes. In the lessons we use explanations, example sentences and exercises. The grammar explanations and exercises on this site (in Turkish) are also freely available for review between lessons.
