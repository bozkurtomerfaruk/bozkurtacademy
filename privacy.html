  • No cookies and no localStorage are used; no identifier is created that recognises you across pages or days.
  • Legal basis: legitimate interest in usage statistics that are not linked to a person, in order to improve the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. Contact form and comments

When you send the contact form or the comment form on the grammar lessons, your name, email address and message are forwarded via FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) to the teacher’s Gmail account. For approved comments, only the name, date and comment are published; the email address is never published.

5. Student account

The student account is used to run the lessons and track your progress. Only the necessary data are collected:

  • full name, email address, year of birth (not the full date of birth),
  • account status (pending approval, active, inactive), approval date and whether the email address has been verified,
  • results of the exercises you complete while signed in: topic, level, score and time,
  • the status of your homework and the Google Docs link you add to a homework task,
  • lesson details: date, time, duration, lesson note and Google Meet link,
  • for students under 16, also a parent’s name and email address and the date and method of the parent’s consent.

Legal basis: performance of the lesson contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). For students under 16, the parent’s consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 8 GDPR). For technical measures such as keeping deletion records and avoiding sending the same email twice: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

After registration, the account is opened once the teacher approves it. Nobody except the teacher can see another student’s data.

5a. Placement test

When you take the placement test and send your result, the following is stored: your name, email address, goal, the name and date of your exam if any, how long you have been learning German, your note, your test result (estimated level, correct answers per part, errors by topic, the questions you answered incorrectly and your answers), the text you wrote in the writing task, the duration of the test and the date you sent it.

I use this information only to plan lessons and to contact you about your first lesson; only I can see it.

The data are processed with Google Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC) and stored in Cloud Firestore on servers in Frankfurt (europe-west3). To send them, a temporary anonymous session is opened via Firebase Authentication and closed immediately afterwards; during this, Google processes your device’s IP address and browser details, possibly also in the USA (EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses). Firebase’s program files are loaded from gstatic.com.

Legal basis: steps taken at your request prior to entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and the consent you give when sending (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

Retention: the result is deleted automatically six months after it was sent. If we start lessons, the relevant details may be transferred to your student account; section 10 then applies.

If you would like your entry deleted earlier, just write to bozkurtt.omerfaruk@gmail.com. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

6. Firebase (Google)

  • Login, student and placement test data are processed with Google Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC).
  • Cloud Firestore: student and placement test data are stored on servers in Frankfurt (europe-west3).
  • Firebase Authentication: The login account (email address, a hash of the password and, when signing in with Google, the name, email address and profile picture address provided by Google) may also be processed by Google outside the EU, in particular in the USA. Transfers to the USA are based on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.
  • When you register with email and password, Firebase sends you an email so that you can verify your email address.
  • Firebase stores your sign-in state in your browser (IndexedDB). This is technically necessary for signing in to work.
  • Signing in with Google is only for students aged 16 and over. The website uses only your name and email address from your Google account.

7. Google Calendar and Google Meet

Each lesson is created as an event in the teacher’s Google Calendar, and a Google Meet link is added automatically. For the invitation, the student’s name, email address and lesson time are passed to Google; for students under 16 (if the setting is on), the parent’s email address is also added to the invitation. Google sends the invitation and update emails. During the call, Google Meet’s own privacy terms apply.

8. Reminder emails

One day before a lesson, the student (and the parent, if applicable) receives a reminder email via Gmail, sent by a Google Apps Script running in the teacher’s Google account. To avoid sending the same email twice, a cryptographic hash (SHA-256) of the email address is stored for 14 days.

9. Students under 16

  • Accounts of students under 16 are not opened without the teacher’s approval and a parent’s consent. Because only the year of birth is known, students who turn 16 this year also need a parent’s consent.
  • The parent’s consent is obtained outside the website (signed form or the parent’s email). The website only records the date and method of consent. Signed consent forms are kept until consent is withdrawn or the student is deleted: paper forms in a locked cabinet, digital ones (scanned form or the parent’s email) in an encrypted folder.
  • The parent’s name and email address are stored for the consent and (if the setting is on) for lesson invitations.
  • A parent can withdraw consent at any time. If consent is withdrawn, all of the student’s data are deleted. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it.

10. Retention period

  • Student data are deleted 12 months after the last lesson (if there was no lesson, 12 months after the account was approved).
  • Registrations that are not approved are deleted within 30 days.
  • Deletion is carried out by the teacher. Afterwards, sent emails in Gmail and events in Google Calendar’s bin (at most 30 days) are also cleared.
